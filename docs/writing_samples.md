## Eviti Connect Help Center
**NantHealth | Web-based help system**

[View the Eviti Connect Help Center](https://nanthealth.com/help/eviti/connect/)

Eviti Connect is an oncology clinical decision support platform used by providers and payers nationwide. As sole writer, I developed and maintained the complete help center for this product, including task-based content for oncology providers submitting treatment plans, and payers reviewing and processing them.

**Highlights:**
- Dual-audience information architecture serving providers and payers
- Task-based topics covering complex clinical and administrative workflows
- Authored in DITA/XML using Oxygen XML Author
- Published via Oxygen XML WebHelp (HTML5 responsive output)
